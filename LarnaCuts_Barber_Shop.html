Our Story

Craftsmanship in Every Cut

LarnaCuts Barber Shop is Larnaca's destination for men who appreciate the art of grooming. Nestled between Droshia Post Office and New York Sweets, we've built our reputation one perfect cut at a time.

Our skilled barbers combine classic techniques with contemporary style — delivering everything from precision fades to traditional hot towel shaves. No appointment needed — just walk in and experience the difference.
