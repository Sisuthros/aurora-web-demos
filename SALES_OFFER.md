## Offer

### 48-Hour Website Rescue

**Audience:** Cyprus-based barbers, restaurants, salons, clinics, tradespeople, and other local businesses whose current website is missing, broken, slow, difficult to use on mobile, or does not make contacting the business easy.

**Promise:** A clean, mobile-first one-page website built and launched within 48 hours after the business approves the content and supplies the required access.

## Founder pricing

- First five customers: **€149 one-time**
- Standard setup after validation: **€249 one-time**
- Optional managed hosting and small edits: **€19/month**

Do not discount below €149. A free personalized concept may be used as proof, but the production build, domain connection, revisions, and launch are paid work.

## Included

- mobile-first one-page website
- clear call, WhatsApp, booking, directions, or enquiry action
- services or menu section
- opening hours and location
- real customer reviews supplied or approved by the business
- basic accessibility and performance pass
- privacy/contact notice appropriate to the page
- domain connection or clean hosted URL
- one revision round
- launch proof on desktop and mobile

## Not included

- fabricated reviews, ratings, visitor counts, awards, or partnerships
- online store, custom booking backend, or large content migration
- logo design or professional photography
- ongoing unlimited changes
- use of third-party photos without permission or a valid licence

## Guarantee

The business pays the setup fee only after approving the final preview for launch. If the delivered page does not match the approved scope, the launch is cancelled and no setup fee is collected.

This does not mean speculative work is unlimited. One concept and one revision round are included.

## Permission-first demo rule

Personalized concepts must be handled as private sales material, not public marketing pages.

- no indexing
- no fabricated reviews or business claims
- no impersonation of the business
- clearly label the page `Independent redesign concept — not the official website`
- use only public factual information or explicit placeholders
- do not use protected logos, customer photos, or copyrighted menu images without permission
- remove immediately on request
- expire unaccepted demos after seven days
- never contact the same business repeatedly after a decline
