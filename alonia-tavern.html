Our Story

A Tradition of Celebration

For years, families in Livadia and Larnaca have chosen Alonia for their most important evenings. There's a reason for that.

Alonia Tavern was born from a simple idea: some evenings are too important to leave to chance. Birthdays, anniversaries, family gatherings, business celebrations — the moments that matter deserve more than just a meal. They deserve a table where everyone feels at home.

Our kitchen specialises in the dishes that bring people together. The slow-cooked lamb kleftiko that fills the room with its aroma. The meze that arrives in thirty small plates until the table disappears under food. The village wine that loosens tongues and warms hearts.

With our #1 TripAdvisor ranking for special occasions, we've become the tavern that Larnaca trusts when something matters. Not because we're fancy — but because we're generous, warm, and we treat every table like family.
