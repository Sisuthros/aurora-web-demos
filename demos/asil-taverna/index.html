Our Story

Three generations around the grill.

What started as a small kitchen serving neighbours has grown into a gathering place for locals, travellers, and anyone who believes food tastes better when shared.

Every dish is made from recipes passed down — souvlaki, grilled halloumi, tahini, fresh village salads, and warm pitta baked in-house.
