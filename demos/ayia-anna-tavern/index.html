Our Story

Where the Mountain Sings and the Table Feeds

High in the hills above Larnaca, Ayia Anna Tavern serves the kind of food that makes you forget about time.

Ayia Anna Tavern sits in a mountain village just 20 minutes from Larnaca, where the air is cooler, the nights are quieter, and the stars seem close enough to touch. We've been serving village-style cuisine for years — the kind of food that travels through generations, not trends.

Our kitchen follows the rhythm of the seasons. Slow-cooked meats that simmer for hours, hand-rolled pastries, village bread baked in a wood-fired oven, and meze that arrives in wave after wave until the table groans. Everything comes from local producers — the olive oil, the wine, the vegetables.

On weekends, the tavern comes alive with live traditional music. The bouzouki starts, the wine flows, and before long, strangers are dancing together. That's the Ayia Anna way — not just a meal, but an evening you'll remember.

Live Music

Weekends Come Alive

Every Friday and Saturday night, the tavern fills with the sound of bouzouki and traditional Cypriot music. Locals and visitors share tables, wine flows freely, and before the night is over, someone always starts to dance.

This isn't a performance — it's a gathering. The musicians sit among the guests. The line between audience and participant blurs. That's the village way.
