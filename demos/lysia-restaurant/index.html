Our Story

A stolen lamb,
a village secret for 34 years.

In 1991, in the quiet hill village of Pyla, our grandfather sealed the first lamb in a clay pot and buried it in the embers of the oven before dawn. By the time the village woke, the meat fell off the bone — tender, fragrant with mountain herbs, and impossible to resist. The legend of the kleftimo — the "stolen" lamb — was born.

Thirty-four years later, we still cook it the same way: sealed in clay, slow-roasted for eight hours, seasoned only with sea salt, oregano from the hills, and a handful of wild bay leaves. No shortcuts. No compromise. Just patience, fire, and the recipe our family has guarded across three generations.
