Our Story

Coastal Village Soul on a Plate

Nestled in Oroklini village, just minutes from the Larnaca coastline, Voreas Tavern brings the warmth of Cypriot hospitality to every table.

Voreas Tavern has been a beloved gathering place in Oroklini for locals and visitors alike. Our name — Voreas, meaning "north wind" — reflects the refreshing breeze that carries the scent of the sea to our doorstep.

Our kitchen is built on three pillars: fresh seafood from the Larnaca bay, charcoal-grilled meats seasoned with mountain herbs, and meze that arrives in wave after wave until your table groans with abundance.

With 236 TripAdvisor reviews and a 94% recommendation rate, we've earned the trust of both locals and travellers. No pretence, no fusion — just honest Cypriot cooking served with a smile and a glass of local wine.
