Our Story

The Heart of Vasileos Pavlou Square

A kafeneio is more than a coffee house — it is the living room of the neighbourhood. We have been pouring traditional Cypriot coffee on this square for over a decade.

To Taxidromio sits on Vasileos Pavlou Square in Larnaca, one of the most charming corners of the old town. Our name means "the post office" — a nod to the square's history as a gathering point for travellers.

We serve traditional Cypriot coffee brewed in the old way: finely ground, slowly heated in a briki, and poured into small cups with a rich foam layer. Pair it with loukoumades, baklava, or a morning meze platter.

With 3,304 Facebook followers and daily posts about our specials, we have become a hub for locals and visitors. Every Friday and Saturday, live music turns the square into something magical.

Coffee, Meze & Music

From dawn coffee to late-night meze — a day at the kafeneio unfolds in stages.

Traditional Cypriot Coffee

Finely ground coffee brewed in a briki. Choose glyko (sweet), metrio (medium), or sketo (plain). Served with a glass of water.

Morning Meze Platter

Feta, olives, pita bread, fresh tomatoes, cucumber, and a boiled egg. The traditional Cypriot start to the day.

€6

Evening Wine & Meze

Local Cypriot wine paired with halloumi, lountza, sheftalia, and village bread. Live music on weekends.
