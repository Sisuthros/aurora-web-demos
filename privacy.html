Privacy

Short, readable, and local-first.

This page fits on one screen because there is very little to hide. No trackers, no cookies banner, no data brokerage.

No tracking pixels · no analytics

01What we collect

The only personal data Aurora Web receives is what you type yourself when you contact us: your name, email address, phone number, and the content of your message. Nothing else is collected — there are no tracking pixels, no analytics scripts, no advertising cookies, and no third-party trackers on this site.

02Where it goes

Your message is delivered straight to our mailbox or WhatsApp. We read it, reply to it, and keep it in our inbox for as long as the conversation is active. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone — ever. There is no CRM export, no lead-list resale, no "carefully selected partners."

03What we build runs local-first

  • Client sites we deliver load fonts and assets directly — no third-party font CDN harvesting visitor data.
  • Our own tools (such as the CRA 24h Clock) run entirely in your browser and phone home to nobody.
  • We never add analytics or tracking pixels to a client site unless the client explicitly asks for them.

04Demos and placeholders

The business demos on this site are original concept sites. Prices, opening hours, reviews, and photos in them are placeholders, not real business data — each demo says so on its own page.

05Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct, or delete anything you have sent us — just reply to any of our emails or message us on WhatsApp. Under GDPR (EU 2016/679) you also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. We handle requests within 30 days, and deletion is immediate on request since the data lives in one mailbox.

06Changes to this page

If this policy ever materially changes, we will update this page with a new revision date rather than silently editing it.
