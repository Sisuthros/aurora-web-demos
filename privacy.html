Privacy & data.

Last updated 4 August 2026

This page is a portfolio site for Aurora Web. It has no accounts, no shopping cart, no analytics and no tracking cookies. What follows is the complete list of what happens to data when you visit.

Who is responsible

Aurora Web — Ville Myllyniemi, operating between Helsinki, Finland and Larnaca, Cyprus. For any question about data on this site, or to ask for a copy or deletion of anything you have sent us, write to viltsu.myllyniemi@gmail.com.

What this site collects

Nothing directly. There is no database and no server-side form handler.

  • The email field on the landing page does not transmit anything to us. It opens a pre-filled message in your own email program, which you choose to send or discard. Until you press send in your own mail app, we never see the address.
  • The WhatsApp buttons open a conversation in WhatsApp. From that point the conversation is processed by Meta under Meta's own terms.
  • No cookies are set by this site. No advertising or analytics scripts are loaded.

Third parties your browser contacts

Loading this page makes your browser request files from the services below. Each of them necessarily receives your IP address and basic request headers, as is the case with any resource fetched over the internet.

  • GitHub Pages (Microsoft) — hosting. Keeps standard server logs.
  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com) — typefaces.
  • Unsplash (images.unsplash.com) — photography used in the page header.
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare) and unpkg — the animation libraries used for scrolling effects.
If you would prefer none of these external requests to happen, they can all be removed by self-hosting the fonts, images and scripts. On client projects that is the default for anyone who asks for it.

Demo pages

The demo sites linked from the portfolio are unsolicited concept work built from publicly available information. They are not the official websites of the businesses shown, none of those businesses is a client of Aurora Web, and none has endorsed this work. Names, prices, opening hours, reviews and photographs on those pages are placeholders and must not be relied on. If a business would like its demo removed, one email to the address above is enough and it comes down.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask what personal data we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected or erased, and object to its processing. Since this site stores nothing, such a request normally concerns only an email or WhatsApp conversation you started with us. We answer within 30 days.

You also have the right to complain to a supervisory authority — in Cyprus, the Office of the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection; in Finland, the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman.
