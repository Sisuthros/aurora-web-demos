2. What data, and where it came from (GDPR Art. 14)

We process publicly available business contact details: business name, sector, address or city, published phone number and public social media pages.

Sources: the business's own public website, its public Facebook or Instagram page, and public business directories. For every contact detail we store the source URL, the time it was retrieved and a SHA-256 hash of the page, so we can tell you exactly where it came from. We do not buy lists and we never guess phone numbers.

3. Purpose and legal basis

Purpose: to tell a business that we have built it a free demo website, and to offer web development. Legal basis: legitimate interest (GDPR 6(1)(f)) — direct marketing to existing businesses. A documented balancing test exists.

Electronic direct marketing (WhatsApp, email, SMS, social DM) in Cyprus is sent only with prior consent (N. 112(I)/2004 art. 106(1), K.Δ.Π. 34/2005). Without consent we contact businesses only by human phone call, post, or by visiting in person.

4. Demo pages

A demo page is built from public information. Every page carries a noindex directive and is not indexed by search engines. It does not claim to be the business's own site. We remove a demo page on request within 24 hours — one message or call is enough. After removal the address returns 404.

5. Retention

Contact details are deleted 12 months after the last contact, or immediately on request. A do-not-contact entry is never deleted — it is the only way to guarantee we do not contact you again.

6. Recipients
